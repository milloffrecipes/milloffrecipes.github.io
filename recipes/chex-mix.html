Gramma's Chex Mix

Gramma's take on Chex Mix.

Prep10 min
Cook1 hour
Serves12 servings
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 1/4 margarine or butter
  • 5 tsp Worcestershire Sauce (we use Lea & Perrin's)
  • 1 1/4 seasoned salt (we use Lawry's)
  • 1/4 garlic powder
  • 2 2/3 Corn Chex cereal
  • 2 2/3 Rice Chex cereal
  • 2 2/3 Wheat Chex cereal
  • 2 cup mixed nuts (we often just use peanuts)
  • 1 pretzels
  • Note: any type of Chex cereal works, as long as a total of 8 cups of Chex are used. We like to sweeten up the mix with some Honey Nut Chex.

Steps

  1. Melt margarine/butter in an open roasting pan in preheated 250°F oven. Stir in seasongs. Rebecca measures seasonings with her heart, Chris measures them out.
  2. Gradually add cereals, nuts and pretzels. Stir to coat evenly.
  3. Bake mix for 1 hour, stirring every 15 minutes.
  4. Spread on paper towels to cool. Store in sealed container (or bag).
Notes: This doesn't last long in our house. Make two batches!
