Pork Schnitzel

A delicious german-style pork schnitzel, best served with egg noodles.

Prep10 min
Cook12 min
Makes6 to 8 servings

Ingredients

  • 6 boneless top pork loin chops (1/2 inch thick, 1 1/2 lbs), trimmed or pork tenderloin cut into 8 pieces
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup dried bread crumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 3 tbps oil

Steps

  1. Place chops between 2 sheets of waxed or parchment paper. Using meat mallet or rolling pin, pound to 1/8 in thick. Sprinkle both sides with salt.
  2. Measure flour and bread crumbs into separate trays (or sheets of waxed paper)
  3. Whisk together egg and milk in tray or bowl
  4. Lightly coat cutlets in flour, shaking off excess, dip into egg/milk mixture, then into bread crumbs, pressing crumbs in to coat cutlet.
  5. Heat oil in large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. Working in batches, add cutlets to skillet, cook, turning once, until both sides are golden brown, approximately 3 minutes per side.
  7. Remove cutlet to warm platter, and serve immediately with a slice of lemon.
  8. If cooking a double or triple batch, place finished schnitzel on tray in oven to keep warm at low temp.
Notes: Pairs great with egg noodles. When feeding a crowd, use pork tenderloins and cut into approximately 8 pieces each before pounding into cutlets.
