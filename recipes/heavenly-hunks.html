Heavenly Hunks

A copycat recipe of the gluten-free snack.

Prep15 min
Freeze1 hour
Serves16 servings

Ingredients

  • 2.5 cups of rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup sweetened coconut, packed
  • 2 tbsp potato starch (you can sub arrowroot, tapioca or cornstarch here - although I've never tried those)
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/8 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup coconut oil
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 1/3 cup chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Add oats, coconut, potato starch, brown sugar and salt to a food processor. Pulse a few times.
  2. Add coconut oil and honey to a microwave safe bowl and microwave for 30 seconds until warm. Pour into the dry ingredients and mix to combine.
  3. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  4. Transfer to an 8x8 dish, lined with parchment paper. Press down firmly.
  5. Freeze for at least 1 hour. Then remove and, using a sharp knife, cut into 16 squares.
  6. Keep stored in a freezer safe bag in the freezer (or fridge) and enjoy!
Notes: Be prepared for lots of "hunks" jokes by the dads in the house. You may need to play around with the ratios (oats, honey, coconut oil) to make sure it's not too "wet".
