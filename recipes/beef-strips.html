Beef Strips

Beef strips, best served over egg noodles.

Prep20 min
Cook1 hour
Serves6 servings
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 1 lb beef (round) cut into strips
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • vegetable oil
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1/2 medium onion, sliced
  • 1/3 cup red wine vingear
  • 1 bag egg noodles

Steps

  1. In pan, heat oil over low heat.
  2. Add onions and cook until translucent.
  3. Add beef slices and cook until browned on both sides.
  4. Sprinkle flour over meat and stir until well coated.
  5. Pour red wine vinegar over beef. Stir and cook for 5 minutes.
  6. Pour beef broth over and cook with covered lid for about 1 hour.
  7. During last 15 minutes of cooking, boil water and cook egg noodles.
Notes: Serve over noodles, with sliced bread.
