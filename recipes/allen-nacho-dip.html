Allen Nacho Dip

An easy and tasty appetizer, that always wows an audience. Constant requests for this one!

Prep10 min
Serves1 pie plate size dip

Ingredients

  • 1 pkg cream cheese
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • taco seasoning (optional)
  • 1 jar salsa
  • grated cheddar cheese
  • Optional toppings
  • Chopped tomatoes
  • Chopped green peppers
  • Chopped red peppers
  • Chopped green onions

Steps

  1. Combine cream cheese and mayo, and taco seasoning if using. Mix until uniform.
  2. Spread on the bottom of a pie plate.
  3. Layer salsa, cheddar cheese, then optional toppings.
Notes: Serve with nacho chips or wedges of pita bread.
