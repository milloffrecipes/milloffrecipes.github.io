Oma's Roast

A simple, but delicious, roast. Serve with mashed potatoes, and side vegetable.

Prep10 min
Cook90 min
Serves6 servings
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 lb roast (e.g., inside round)
  • 1/3 cup crisco
  • 1 onion, cut into wedges
  • 3 bay leaves
  • paprika
  • salt
  • pepper
  • flour

Steps

  1. In pot, add crisco, wedge onion, bay leaves on med-high heat.
  2. Brown all sides, then add water.
  3. Reduce heat to medium-low. Cook at least 30 min/lb (so approximately 90 minutes)
  4. Cover. Rotate roast frequently. If browning too much, add extra water.
  5. Remove roast and let rest.
  6. While roast is resting, strain onion and bay leaves from remaining liquid. Add flour, whisk, and cook until thickened. Add more water if necessary.
  7. Slice roast into thin slices, and return to gravy pot.
Notes: Serve with mashed potatoes, Oma's creamy cauliflower, corn, carrots, and/or green beans.
