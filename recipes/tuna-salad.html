Tuna Salad

A quick lunch

Prep10 min
Cook10 min
Makes6 servings
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 1¼ lb pasta
  • 4 cans of tuna
  • 4 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 onion diced
  • ¼ cup mayo
  • ¼ cup miracle whip
  • pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Boil pasta for 10 minutes
  2. mix all other ingredients in a bowl
  3. Strain pasta and cool with cold water
  4. mix everything together
