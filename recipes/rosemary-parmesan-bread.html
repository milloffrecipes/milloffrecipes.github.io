Prep20 min Rise1–2 hr Bake35 min Makes1 loaf Ingredients 3½ cups bread flour 1 packet (2¼ tsp) active dry yeast 1½ tsp salt 1 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped ¾ cup grated Parmesan 1¼ cups warm water 2 tbsp olive oil Steps Combine flour, yeast, salt, rosemary, and Parmesan in a large bowl. Stir in warm water and olive oil until a shaggy dough forms. Knead 8–10 minutes, then let rise, covered, until doubled — about 1–2 hours. Shape into a loaf, let rest 20 minutes. Bake at 425°F for about 35 minutes, until deep golden and hollow-sounding when tapped. Cool at least 20 minutes before slicing. Notes: add any substitutions, storage tips, or "next time" notes here.
