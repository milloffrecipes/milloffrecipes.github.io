Oma's Creamy Cauliflower

A creamy cauliflower side dish, that can be made with fresh or frozen cauliflower.

Prep10 min
Cook15 min
Serves6 servings
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cauliflower head (or frozen cauliflower)
  • butter
  • flour
  • reserved water
  • milk
  • nutmeg, to taste
  • salt, to taste

Steps

  1. Steam cauliflower until tender.
  2. Remove cauliflower, and reserve steam water.
  3. Melt butter in saucepot. Add equal parts flour, whisk and cook to create a roux.
  4. Slowly add milk to the roux. Whisk until smooth.
  5. Add in reserved water until desired thickness.
  6. Add in nutmeg, and salt if needed, to the sauce.
  7. Stir in cauliflower, and serve warm.
Notes: Pairs great with Oma's Roast for a full Sunday meal.
