Oma's Hazelnut Cake

Oma's Hazelnut Cake. Be careful with this one or it might come out as Gramma's Hazelnut Soup Cake.

Prep10 min
Bake20 to 30 min
Serves12 servings

Ingredients

  • Cake
  • 200 gm filberts (ground hazelnut)
  • 4 eggs
  • 200 gm sugar
  • 1 tbsp hot water
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • Topping
  • Small tub (500 mL) Cool Whip
  • 1 tsp cocoa
  • 1/2 tsp fine ground coffee

Steps

  1. Beat eggs and sugar together.
  2. Add filbert, baking powder and water. Combine.
  3. Bake at 350°F for 20 to 30 minutes in greased pan.
  4. When cool, combine topping ingredients and spread over cake.
