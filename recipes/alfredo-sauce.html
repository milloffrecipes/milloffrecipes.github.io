Alfredo Sauce

A quick and easy sauce for making fettuccine alfredo, or to use in our cheese cappelletti dish.

Prep10 min
Cook10 min
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cup chicken broth (or water and chicken bouillon)
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 1/2 cup cup cream cheese
  • 3 tbsp grated parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/8 tsp pepper
  • Scale up ingredients based on how thick you like your sauce or how much nutmeg and pepper you want. It becomes a measure with your heart recipe after you make it often enough!

Steps

  1. Combine broth and flour in a medium saucepan.
  2. Stir in cream cheese, parmesan cheese, nutmeg and pepper.
  3. Cook 2 minutes, stirring constantly with wire whisk to remove lumps, until mixture boils and thickens.
  4. Toss pasta with sauce and garnish as desired.
Notes: Great over fettuccine noodles, topped with chicken or as part of the cheese cappelletti meal.
