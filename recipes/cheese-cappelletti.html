Cheese Cappelletti

Just like East Side Mario's

Prep10 minutes
Bake20 minutes
Makes5 servings
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups tomato sauce
  • 1 cup alfredo sauce
  • 500 g fresh tortellini
  • 2 tbsp parmesan cheese
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • 2 tsp olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F
  2. Mix sauces together and heat gently until warmed through
  3. Cook pasta for ~5 minutes. Mix cheeses together
  4. Mix pasta with sauce and coat pasta. Pour into baking dish
  5. Spread cheese mixture onto pasta
  6. Drizzle olive oil over, bake 20 minutes
