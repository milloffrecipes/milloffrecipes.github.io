Gramma's Cinnamon "Roles"

Classic cinnamon rolls.

Prep30 min
Rise3 hours
Bake20 to 25 min
Serves12 servings
Scale:

Ingredients

  • Dough
  • 2 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp yeast
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter (and more for brushing)
  • 1 egg
  • Filling
  • 1/2 cup skim milk
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp minced orange zest
  • Icing
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup orange juice

Steps

  1. Combine flour, yeast, salt, and sugar in a food processor
  2. Add 2 tbsp butter and egg.Pulse a few times to mix.
  3. With machine running, pour most of the milk through tube. Process until a ball forms. Add more milk 1 tsp at a time if needed.
  4. Knead by hand on a lightly floured surface, adding more milk or flour as needed.
  5. Butter a bowl and place dough in it, turning to coat. Cover and let rise in a warm area for 2 hours.
  6. Roll out dough into 1/2 inch thick rectangle. Brush liberally with butter. Spread the filling mixture (sugar, cinnamon and orange zest).
  7. If desired, add raisins or chopped nuts.
  8. Roll up jelly-roll style. Then cut into 1 to 1 1/2 inch thick slices.
  9. Place rolls in a buttered round pan. Place one in the middle and work the others around so that they are touching. If doubling/tripling recipe, use extra round pans.
  10. Cover and let rise until puffy, about 1 hour.
  11. While rolls are rising, boil together sugar and orange juice until thickened.
  12. Just before baking, brush with butter and then orange juice mixture.
  13. Bake at 400°F for 20 to 25 minutes.
