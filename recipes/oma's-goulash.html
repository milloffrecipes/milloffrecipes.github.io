Oma's Goulash

Oma's Goulash. Best served over bucatini noodles, or Owen's favourite - mashed potatoes, and a side salad.

Prep5 min
Cook1 1/2 to 2 hours
Serves6 servings
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 1 lb stewing beef and pork blend
  • salt, pepper
  • 1/2 medium onion, chopped
  • paprika
  • 4 bay leaves
  • 1/2 cup water (add more if required)
  • 2 tbsp flour and additional water (reserve for last step)

Steps

  1. Place stewing beef/pork in frying pan. Add salt and pepper to cover the pot once (slightly less for pepper)
  2. Add paprika, so every piece has some on it.
  3. Add all other ingredients to frying pan.
  4. Cook in covered frying pay for 1 1/2 to 2 hours on low heat until tender.
  5. Make flour-water mixture with 2 tbsp flour and cold water. It should be runnier than a paste.
  6. Add to the frying pan to make a gravy at the end of the cook time.
Notes: Serve with bucatini noodles, or Owens' favourite - mashed potatoes.
