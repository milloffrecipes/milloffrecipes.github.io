Pizza Dough

Chris's homemade pizza dough.

Prep15 min min
Rise1 hour
Bake15-20 min
Serves2 to 3 pizzas

Ingredients

  • 2 cups warm water/li>
  • 1 tbsp active dry yeast
  • 1 1/2 tsp honey
  • 5 cups flour and extra to make ball consistency
  • 3 tsp kosher salt
  • olive oil

Steps

  1. Mix water, yeast and honey in stand mixer mixing bowl. Let sit for 5 minutes.
  2. Add flour and salt to bowl. Using dough hook, mix on speed 2 for 2 minutes.
  3. Add additional flour until dough ball cleans bowl.
  4. Mix for 4 more minutes at speed 3.
  5. Coat another bowl with olive oil.
  6. Place dough in bowl, and cover for at least one hour.
  7. After dough has risen, punch dough down, and turn onto a floured surface. Shape into pizzas (2 to 3 large pizzas, or several small ones). Then top with your favourite sauces and toppings.
  8. Bake at 450°F for 15 to 20 minutes until crust is golden brown. If cooking two pizzas at the same time, rotate racks halfway through.
Notes: Makes good garlic bread as well!
