Gramma's Lasagna

A family favourite lasagna recipe.

Prep40 min
Simmer30 min
Bake30 min
Rest10 min
Makes1 pan

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs ground beef, browned, fat poured off (or 1 lb ground beef, 1 lb italian sausage)
  • 1 large can crushed tomatoes (or 4 cups of tomato sauce)
  • 1 13 oz can tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • Garlic powder (or 5 cloves, minced and 1/2 tbsp garlic powder)
  • 1 tbsp basil
  • 1 tbsp oregano
  • 2 tubs (1 kg) cottage cheese (or ricotta)
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1/2 cup parmesan cheese
  • 1 tsp parsley
  • Oven-ready lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Mix together cooked ground beef, crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, sugar, garlic powder, basil and oregano.
  2. Cook over low heat for 30 minutes.
  3. Mix together cottage cheese, eggs, pepper, parmesan cheese and parsley.
  4. Set aside 1 cup meat mixture.
  5. In a large lasagna pan, layer as follows.
  6. Line bottom of lasagna pan lightly with tomato sauce
  7. Lasagna noodles.
  8. 1/2 meat sauce.
  9. 1/2 cheese mixture.
  10. 1/2 lb shredded mozzarella
  11. Repeat steps 7-10.
  12. Top with reserved meat sauce.
  13. Sprinkle with parmesan cheese.
  14. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes. Let stand for 10 minutes before serving.
Notes: Easy recipe to double, have one for dinner and pop one in the freezer for later! Great meal for new parents too. Please note - this recipe uses both imperial and metric measurements. We know this is wrong, but that's how the recipe has been passed down!
