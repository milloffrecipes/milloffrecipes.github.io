Oma Apple Cake (Cherry Cake)

Prep20 minutes
Bake1 hour
Makes1 cake
Scale:

Ingredients

  • Base
  • 100 g butter, softened
  • 3 eggs
  • 200 g flour
  • 1½ tsp baking powder
  • 200 g sugar
  • Fruit Layer
  • 8 apples cored, peeled and sliced
  • or 2 cans cherries well drained
  • Topping
  • 100 g butter (melted)
  • 100 g sugar
  • 150 g flour
  • 1 package vanilla sugar
  • 1 pinch of cinnamon

Steps

  1. Beat base ingredients well
  2. Pour into a lightly greased spring form pan
  3. Add fruit on top
  4. Mix topping ingredients together and crumble
Notes: Top with whipped cream
