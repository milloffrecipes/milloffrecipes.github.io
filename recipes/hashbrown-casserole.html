Hashbrown Casserole

An Easter Favourite

Prep10 minutes
Bake50 - 55 minutes
Makes2 Quarts
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 1 can cream of chicken soup
  • ⅔ container sour cream
  • 4 cups frozen hashbrown
  • 1 cup diced cooked ham (~125 g)
  • 1 cup cubed cheddar cheese
  • ¼ cup chopped onion
  • ⅛ tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 cup cornflakes
  • 3 tbsp melted butter

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F
  2. Stir together soup and sour cream
  3. Stir in hashbrown, ham cheese, onion, and pepper
  4. Spread into 2 qt. baking dish
  5. Combine cornflakes and butter. Sprinkle over potato mixture
  6. Bake uncovered for 50 - 55 minutes or until hot and bubbly
  7. Let stand 10 minutes before serving
Notes: Substitute shredded cheese for cornflakes and butter
