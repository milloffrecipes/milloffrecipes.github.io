Mrs. Hardy's Chocolate Chip Cookies

A former teacher made these cookies for Rebecca in high school, and it's now a family favourite.

Prep10 min
Bake8 to 10 min per cookie sheet
Serves4 dozen
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2/3 cup margarine/butter
  • 2/3 cup shortening
  • 2 eggs, beaten slightly
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 500 g chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 350°F.
  2. Mix sugars, butter, shortening, eggs and vanilla.
  3. Stir in remaining ingredients.
  4. Drop by rounded spoonfuls onto greased baking sheet.
  5. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, cool slightly before removing from cookie sheet.
  6. Makes 3 to 4 dozen.
Notes: Makes great ice cream cookie sandwiches!
