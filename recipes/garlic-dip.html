Garlic Dipping Sauce

A fresh garlic dipping sauce that pairs well with our pizza dough recipe.

Prep5 min
Serves1 servings
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp pesto (or mix basil, parmesan cheese, garlic and olive oil together)
  • 2 tbsp mayo
  • 1 melted butter
  • 2 minced/microplaned garlic cloves
  • paprika
  • salt
  • pepper
  • flour

Steps

  1. Mix ingredients together. Prepare while pizza is baking. Refrigerate any leftovers.
Notes: Serve with mashed potatoes, Oma's creamy cauliflower, corn, carrots, and/or green beans.
