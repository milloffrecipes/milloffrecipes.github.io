Gigi's Banana Nut Loaf

Ripe bananas? Look no further!

Prep10 min
Bake50 min
Serves1 loaf

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup shortening
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 2 mashed bananas (we often use 3)
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • 1/2 cup cherries and/or nuts (optional)

Steps

  1. Beat shortening and sugar together until light and fluffy.
  2. Sift together flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt.
  3. Add eggs and mashed bananas to sugar mixture.
  4. Slowly add dry ingredients a little at a time, alternating with milk.
  5. Fold in cherries and/or nuts, if using.
  6. Bake in lightly greased loaf pan at 350°F for 50 minutes until toothpick comes out clean.
Notes: For some extra fun, argue about whether or not you should serve with butter.
