Oma's Potato Salad

A simple potato salad recipe, with no quantities. Cook with your heart (and stomach) for this family favourite side dish.

Prep20 min
Serves12 servings unless Chris is around, then it's less

Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Salt
  • Butter
  • Pepper
  • Vinegar
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Mayo

Steps

  1. Cook Potatoes (with salt and butter)
  2. Cool potatoes, peel potatoes once cooled.Don't cut until cooled.
  3. Cut fine.
  4. Salt and Pepper on potatoes after each layer in bowl.
  5. Add white vinegar spoonful to potatoes.
  6. Add 3 spoondfuls of vegetable oil.
  7. Add a spoonful of mayo and mix.
  8. Taste and add mayo, salt, pepper, etc. as needed.
Notes: No one can quite make it like Oma's, but if you do, hopefully you wrote down proper measurements so you can make it again.
