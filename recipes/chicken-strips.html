Breaded Chicken Strips

Homemade breaded chicken strips, with a simple spicy sauce.

Prep15 min
Cook17-19 min
Serves4 servings
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 3 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into strips
  • 1/2 cup bread crumbs
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 tsp paprika, salt, pepper, garlic powder, each don't feel like you need to measure, but just spice to your heart's content
  • Hot Sauce
  • 3/4 hot sauce
  • 5 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp minced garlic or more if you're feeding Rebecca

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line two baking sheets with aluminum foil and spray with cooking spray.
  2. In a shallow bowl or coating tray, beat the eggs.
  3. In another shallow bowl or coating tray, combine the flour, bread crumbs, and spices. Mix until well combined.
  4. Dip the chicken in the egg, coating evenly. Allow excess to drip off before placing in the bread crumb mixture. Coat evenly. Move to baking sheet.
  5. In a shallow bowl or coating tray, beat the eggs.
  6. Repeat for all chicken strips.
  7. Bake for 7-8 minutes and flip. Bake for another 7-8 minutes. Remove and dip into sauce mixture and return to oven for 3-4 more minutes.
  8. To make sauce, combine hot sauce, butter and garlic in a small saucepan and cook on medium-low heat.
Notes: Switch up the sauce for new flavours - try them plain with sweet and sour sauce or ketchup, our honey garlic sauce, bbq sauce, or whatever you feel like!
