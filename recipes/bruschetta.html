Gramma's Bruschetta

Tastes best with tomatoes from the garden on homemade baguettes.

Prep5 min
Serves6 servings, unless Rebecca is there, then you might have to fight her for it

Ingredients

  • No actual measurements - this one is by feel
  • Ripe tomatoes
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Basil best if grown from the garden!
  • Garlic best if grown from the garden!

Steps

  1. Chop tomaotes. Add the rest of the ingredients.
  2. If Rebecca is coming, add more garlic.
  3. Toast bread and serve!
