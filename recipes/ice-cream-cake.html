Ice Cream Cake

Crsipy Crunch Ice Cream Cake, with crispy crunch pieces in, and on top, of the ice cream layer. Make this one ahead of time!

Prep20 min
Freeze4 hours or overnight
Serves12 servings

Ingredients

  • Crust
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 1 1/3 cup oreo baking crumbs
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • Filling
  • 2L premium vanilla ice cream
  • 1/2 cup smooth peanut butter
  • 1 cup roasted salted skinless peanuts
  • 4 bars (192 g) Crispy Crunch (crushed)
  • Topping
  • 1 bar (48 g) Crispy Crunch (crushed)

Steps

  1. Combine melted butter with oreo crumbs and sugar. Press into bottom of springform pan. Place in freezer until hard.
  2. Let ice cream stand at room temperature to soften.
  3. Heat peanut butter in microwave for 20 seconds or until flowing smoothly.
  4. Pour peanut butter on frozen crust, spreading evenly to edges.
  5. Sprinkle peanuts evenly on peanut butter layer. Return to freezer.
  6. Mix crushed Crispy Crunch with ice cream until evenly mixed. Pour over peanut layer and smooth top.
  7. Sprinkle remaining 1 bar of crushed Crispy Crunch on top of ice cream layer. Return to freezer until serving.
Notes: Warm knife in jug of warm water to make slicing easier. You can use the snack size bars of Crispy Crunch, just match weight.
