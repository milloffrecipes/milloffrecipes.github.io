Rosemary Parmesan Bread

A soft, golden yeasted loaf with fresh rosemary and nutty Parmesan baked right into the crust.

Prep20 min
Rise1–2 hr
Bake28-32 min
Makes1 loaf

Ingredients

  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 packet (2¼ tsp) active dry yeast
  • 1 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsb olive oil, plus extra for brushing
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped, divided
  • ¾ cup grated Parmesan, divided
  • ½ tsp coarse seal salt, for topping

Steps

  1. Bloom the yeast Combine water, yeast and sugar in a large bowl. Let sit for 8-10 minutes until foamy.
  2. Mix wet ingredients Whisk in olive oil and egg until combined.
  3. Add dry ingredients Add flour, salt, 2 tbsp rosemary, and 1/2 cup Parmesan. Mix until a shaggy dough forms.
  4. Knead Knead with dough hook or by hand 6-8 min, until smooth, elastic, and springs back when poked.
  5. First rise (45-60 min) Shape into a ball, place in oiled bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm spot until doubled.
  6. Shape the loaf Punch down and shape into a round or oval loaf. Place on parchment-lined sheet or in a greased loaf pan.
  7. Second rise (30-45 min) Cover loosely and let rise again until puffy and nearly doubled.
  8. Preheat oven About 15 minutes before second rise ends, preheat oven to 375°F.
  9. Top the loaf Brush top with olive oil. Sprinkle with remaining rosemary, remaining Parmesan, and coarse salt. Score if desired.
  10. Bake (28-32 min) Bake until a deep golden brown and loaf sounds hollow when tapped.
  11. Cool (20 min) Cool on a wire rack at least 20 minutes before slicing.
Notes: Works beautifully as focaccia — press into an oiled pan, dimple with fingers, top the same way. Store wrapped at room temp 2 days, or freeze sliced up to 3 months
