Beef Stew

A slow-cooked beef stew, great for cold days. Affectionately called fart stew!

Prep10 min
Cook6 to 8 hours
Serves6 servings
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 2 lb stewing beef
  • 1 can beef broth
  • 1 can whole potatoes
  • 1 can whole carrots
  • 1 package onion soup mix
  • 4 tbsp flour

Steps

  1. Combine all but carrots and flour in a slow cooker.
  2. Put on low heat for 6 to 8 hours.
  3. Add carrots during the last two hours, reserving the carrot liquid from the can.
  4. Strain liquid in slow cooker to remove onions.
  5. Stir flour into carrot liquid until combined. Add to slow cooker, and heat for another 1/2 hour in stew.
Notes: Serve with fresh bread for dipping.
