Rosemary Parmesan Bread

Prep20 min
Rise1–2 hr
Bake35 min
Makes1 loaf

Ingredients

  • 3½ cups bread flour
  • 1 packet (2¼ tsp) active dry yeast
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
  • ¾ cup grated Parmesan
  • 1¼ cups warm water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Steps

  1. Combine flour, yeast, salt, rosemary, and Parmesan in a large bowl.
  2. Stir in warm water and olive oil until a shaggy dough forms.
  3. Knead 8–10 minutes, then let rise, covered, until doubled — about 1–2 hours.
  4. Shape into a loaf, let rest 20 minutes.
  5. Bake at 425°F for about 35 minutes, until deep golden and hollow-sounding when tapped.
  6. Cool at least 20 minutes before slicing.
