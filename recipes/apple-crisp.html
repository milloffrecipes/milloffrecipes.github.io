Gigi's Apple Crisp

A favourite fall classic.

Prep5 min
Bake30 min
Serves6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 4-5 medium apples (we use whatever we pick at the orchard)

Steps

  1. Slice apples into a foil-lined baking pan.
  2. Mix brown sugar, flour and oats in a separate bowl. Sprinkle on top of apples.
  3. Melt butter and pour over mixture.
  4. Bake in oven 350°F for 1/2 hour
Notes: Can be frozen before being baked. It can be popped into the oven when frozen, it will just need to take longer to cook. A great way to use the apples from an apple picking day!
