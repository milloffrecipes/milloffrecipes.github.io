Oma Cake

A constant in Oma's kitchen, this simple cake is easy to make. Great for afternoon coffee and cake! Be prepared for a fight over the crumbs once the cake is finished.

Prep10 min
Bake30 min
Serves12 servings

Ingredients

  • 200 gm Butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 200 gm sugar
  • 1 package vanilla sugar
  • 250 gm flour
  • 2 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • icing sugar and milk for icing

Steps

  1. Beat butter, eggs and sugar together until fluffy.
  2. Add vanilla sugar, flour and baking powder to wet ingredients and beat well.
  3. Bake in 350°F oven for 30 minutes.
  4. Once cake is cooled, mix together icing sugar and milk, and drizzle over cake.
Notes: Make in bundt pan. Can add sprinkles for an extra splash of fun.
