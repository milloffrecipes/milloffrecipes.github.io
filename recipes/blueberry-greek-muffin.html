Blueberry Greek Yogurt Muffins

Light and fluffy blueberry greek yogurt muffins, using fresh or frozen blueberries.

Prep15 min
Cook18-20 min
Serves18 muffins
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda)
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 2 large eggs)
  • 1 cup plain Greek Yogurt
  • 1 1/2 unsalted butter, melted and cooled
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • zest of 1 lemon
  • 1.5 cups fresh or frozen blueberries (don't thaw if frozen) this is about 1 pint minus a small handful
  • 2 tbsp coarse or turbinado sugar, for topping

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line two muffin tins with paper liners or spray with cooking spray.
  2. Take 2 tbsp of the flour and toss it with the blueberries in a small bowl. This keeps them from sinking to the bottom.
  3. In a large bow, whisk together the remaining flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  4. In a separate bowl, whisk the eggs until smooth, then whisk in the yogurt, melted butter, oil, milk, vanilla, and lemon zest until combined.
  5. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients. Fold gently with a spatula until just combined, a few streaks of flour are fine, don't overmix. Fold in the floured blueberries, saving a small handful for the tops.
  6. Divide the batter evenly among 18 muffin cups, filling almost to the top. Press the reserved blueberries onto the tops, and sprinkle each muffin with coarse sugar.
  7. Bake at 425°F for 5 minutes. After the five minutes, reduce heat to 350°F. Continue baking 13-15 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean and the tops spring back.
  8. Let cool in tins for 5 minutes, then move to a wire cooling rack.
Notes: Keeps 2 days on the counter, up to a week in the fridge, or 3 months frozen. Frozen berries work fine, just don't thaw them first so they don't streak the batter.
