Gramma's Waffles

Light and fluffy waffles, ready for lots of variation.

Prep10 min
Cook5 min per batch
Serves12 rectangular waffles (as waffle makers vary, this will impact how many the recipe makes)
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 2 egg whites, stifly beaten
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1/3 cup oil
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tbsp baking powder

Steps

  1. Preheat waffle maker.
  2. Mix all but egg whites in large bowl.
  3. Fold in stifly beaten egg whites.
  4. Add 1/3 cup batter to waffle maker (per rectangular square in a four rectangle waffle maker). You will need to adjust for your waffle maker.
  5. Bake for 5 minutes.
  6. Remove to wire rack.
  7. Variations: Add chocolate chips or blueberries before baking. You can also add protein powder to the mix, swap out for equal parts flour.
Notes: Serve with strawberry sauce, whip cream, powdered sugar, syrup or just on their own. We often make waffle bits by tearing up finshed waffles into bite size pieces for a snack too.
