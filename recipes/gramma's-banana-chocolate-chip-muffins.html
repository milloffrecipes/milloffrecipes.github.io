Gramma's Banana Chocolate Chip Muffins

A quick and delicious banana chocolate chip muffin recipe.

Prep10 min
Bake20 min
Makes12 muffins
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 egg well beaten
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Mash the bananas and add the oil, sugar and salt. Beat until blended.
  2. Add the egg and vanilla and beat again.
  3. Sift the flour, baking soda and baking powder into the banana mixture.
  4. Add the chocolate chips and stir to moisten the flour.
  5. Drop into greased (or papered) muffin tins and bake for 20 minutes in a 350°F oven.
  6. Makes 12 regular size muffin, or 6 large.
Notes: Top with turbinado sugar for an extra treat!
