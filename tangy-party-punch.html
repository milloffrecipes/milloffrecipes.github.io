Tangy Party Punch

Prep5 minutes
Makes8 Quarts
Scale:

Ingredients

  • 48 fl. oz. pineapple juice
  • 48 fl. oz. orange juice
  • 12 fl. oz. frozen limeade
  • 12 fl. oz. frozen lemonade
  • 3 L ginger ale
  • 1 Pt. each orange, lemon, and lime sherbet

Steps

  1. Just before serving, combine all ingredients but sherbet, in a large punchbowl
  2. Add scoops of the sherbet to the punch
